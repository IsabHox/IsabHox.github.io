I just spent three weeks, from February 16th to March 6th 2026, in London.

As part of an ongoing project, I am working on Disentangled Recurrent Neural Networks, a method developped by people at DeepMind, in particular Kevin Miller, with whom I have the priviledge to work. I was also partially hosted at the Sainsbury Wellcome Center, in Tom Msric-Flogel's team. 

This trip was also the occasion to meet with other London-based teams of the International Brain Lab, in particular Kenneth Harris' team, with whom I spent an afternoon.

London is also home for many academics that I met throughout my academic experiences, so it was nice to reconnect with this community!
